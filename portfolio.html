01 / 08Propulsion & test · 2024-present

Propulsion engineering across industry

Mechanical design, thermo-mechanical analysis and test development across HURACAN, Orbex Prime propulsion systems and a Hall-effect thruster demonstrator.

ROLEMechanical Design Engineer, The Exploration Company; Propulsion Test and Design Engineer, Orbex; Consultant, Pulsar Fusion

THE CHALLENGE

Move propulsion hardware and test infrastructure through design definition, verification, manufacturing and integrated test while managing tight interfaces and test-readiness needs.

My contribution

  • HURACAN / Component design - responsible for component design and thermo-mechanical verification across injector head, torch igniter, gimbal mount, TVC brackets and thrust structure. Designed the first TVC actuator brackets, defining trade-offs, load cases and boundary conditions through bench integration.
  • HURACAN / Structural and thermal analysis - Established the first DM2 engine-level structural FEM in ANSYS; analysis findings informed redesign of engine-pump interfaces. Identified and corrected an igniter heat-transfer assumption using the Bartz correlation, improving agreement with the 1D thermal model.
  • HURACAN / Interfaces and alignment - Developed margin-of-safety plotting and Siemens NX workflows; owned geometric definition and tolerance stack-ups, reviewed CMM data, and introduced interface prototyping and precision alignment pins. The pin change improved alignment by 34%.
  • HURACAN / Integrated hot-fire - Contributed to the integrated HURACAN hot-fire campaign with flight-like 50-100% throttle sweeps in Q2 2026. The 15 kN methane electric-pump-fed engine is progressing toward planned integrated lander hopper testing in 2027.
  • ORBEX / Test infrastructure - led mechanical and fluidic design of the Copenhagen Stage 1 Engine Powerpack Test Facility; supported turbopump and test-bench design/analysis, live cryogenic LOX/BioLPG testing, anomaly resolution and RCS development using RPA and Simcenter Amesim.
  • PULSAR / In-space demonstrator - supported a 1 kW Hall-effect thruster in-orbit demonstrator payload from concept toward manufacturing readiness and mentored junior engineers.

OUTCOME

At HURACAN, structural and thermal findings drove design updates and the team completed an integrated hot-fire campaign. Across the Orbex and Pulsar roles, work ranged from propulsion test infrastructure and live test support to small-spacecraft propulsion-payload maturity.

02 / 08Space hardware · 2022-2025

Motion systems for environmental testing

Two ESA ESTEC mechanisms developed for payload motion and instrumentation in thermal-vacuum facilities: the VTC1.5 translation stage and COLIBRIS.

ROLELead Design Engineer, VTC1.5; Principal Design Engineer, COLIBRIS

THE CHALLENGE

Deliver repeatable, remotely commanded motion for measurement hardware operating within large thermal-vacuum test facilities, while meeting mass, configuration and manufacturing constraints.

My contribution

  • VTC1.5 / Design and qualification - progressed the two-axis X/Y system from concept through subsystem design, build, integration and TVAC qualification; completed structural and thermoelastic assessments and supported campaign execution.
  • VTC1.5 / Payload performance - The VTC1.5 mechanism repositioned a 50 kg test payload with 0.5 mm spatial resolution. Post-test findings informed a cable-tensioning design modification.
  • COLIBRIS / Design ownership - took principal design ownership of a 3 m CFRP motion system for the Large Space Simulator, from concept through CDR and manufacturing in nine months. The design supports in-situ calibration of two photogrammetry cameras and infrared thermal measurements.
  • COLIBRIS / Mass and metrology - Optimised the photogrammetric network within manufacturing constraints; final design mass was 33.08 kg, 57% below the allowable hanging mass. The configuration supports more accurate measurement of spacecraft thermo-elastic deformation.

OUTCOME

VTC1.5 completed qualification and repositioned its 50 kg payload in vacuum at -193 C. The work was presented at ECSSMET in 2024; COLIBRIS was presented at the 2025 Aerospace Testing Seminar.

03 / 08Space hardware · 2022-2024

Flight payload hardware: YPSAT and AstroBit

Mechanical design, integration and environmental-test work for two ESA payloads that reached space: YPSAT on Ariane 6 and AstroBit on the International Space Station.

ROLEStructural-design contributor, YPSAT; Lead Design Engineer, AstroBit

THE CHALLENGE

Deliver compact payload hardware that could be assembled, verified and flown within spacecraft and launch-interface constraints.

My contribution

  • YPSAT / Structural design and integration - co-led structural design and final component delivery; authored electro-mechanical assembly procedures and supported cleanroom integration.
  • YPSAT / Environmental testing - Defined vibration test profiles, developed notching approaches and processed test data during qualification and acceptance campaigns; also designed a shipping container for transport to Kourou.
  • ASTROBIT / Flight case - led mechanical concept development, design, build and test of a flight case for an educational micro:bit payload. The design kept USB and 2 x AA battery access while maintaining a flight-case mass below 0.8 kg.

OUTCOME

YPSAT flew on Ariane 6's maiden flight in July 2024 and achieved its scientific objectives. AstroBit was operated aboard the ISS by ESA astronaut Andreas Mogensen during the Huginn mission in 2023.

04 / 08Industrial design · 2019-2020

Concrete Canvas: production equipment redesign

Mechanical R&D across legacy machine upgrades and new production-line hardware, with calculations, CAD/FEA, test-jig development and hands-on fabrication.

ROLEResearch and Development Engineer Intern

THE CHALLENGE

Improve the capacity and reliability of production-support equipment while working through structural limits, uncertain loads, manufacturing fit and schedule constraints.

My contribution

  • Accumulator / Reverse engineering and analysis - reverse-engineered an existing machine assembly from drawings and measurement, then used free-body diagrams, beam-bending calculations and SolidWorks Simulation to identify high-load components.
  • Accumulator / Test verification - Designed a test jig and verified carriage-lift calculations using incremental loading; measured results agreed with the theoretical estimate to within 25 kg on a 400 kg test load.
  • Accumulator / Redesign and implementation - Redesigned the load-bearing bracket and roller components; prepared CAD/GD&T drawings, coordinated suppliers and supported fabrication and fit-up. The accumulator upgrade increased lifting capacity by 100% to 25 kN at 30% of replacement cost.
  • Production equipment / Hopper and extrusion line - Designed and sized elements of a 750 L LDPE blend hopper, including motor/gearbox selection and the torque-arm mounting plate. Reconstructed a full extrusion-machine CAD assembly in four weeks from drawings and hand measurements.
  • Manufacturing technology / Iterative builds - Delivered five design-build-test cycles related to new manufacturing technology and completed hands-on assembly and production-line integration support.

OUTCOME

The placement combined analytical design with measured validation and implementation: a major accumulator-capacity upgrade, fabricated replacement components and production-line equipment models. The results demonstrate transferable design, analysis, supplier and workshop skills.
