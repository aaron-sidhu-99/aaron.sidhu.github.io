# Portfolio Project Inventory & Recommended Structure

## Executive recommendation

Keep the portfolio focused: eight top-level case studies are enough to tell the story without making every short assignment compete for equal attention. The site now uses four browsing themes—**Space hardware**, **Propulsion & test**, **Industrial design**, and **Academic & concepts**—and groups closely related work beneath them.

For the homepage, lead with four proof points:

1. **Industry propulsion** — current, technically deep, and the strongest signal for propulsion roles.
2. **ESA motion systems** — a strong end-to-end mechanical-design and qualification story.
3. **Flight payloads** — shows work connected to hardware that reached space.
4. **Concrete Canvas** — adds industrial R&D, practical implementation, and clear quantified results.

Keep the remaining university and concept projects available in the portfolio, but give them less visual weight than professional work. For a propulsion-focused application, feature **C-Star** in place of one of the four homepage cards.

## Project inventory and recommended treatment

### 1. HURACAN — 15 kN lunar-lander engine
**Priority:** Flagship case study, first in the propulsion story.

Emphasize component design and thermo-mechanical verification across the injector head, torch igniter, gimbal mount, TVC brackets, and thrust structure; the engine-level structural FEM; the igniter heat-transfer correction using the Bartz correlation; the reported **34% interface-alignment improvement**; and the Q2 2026 hot-fire throttle sweeps. Keep the story centered on your specific design, analysis, and verification contribution, with the broader engine milestone as context.

**Recommended visuals:** a clear project overview graphic until a project-specific image is selected, plus any authorized design, analysis, integration, or test image that can be captioned precisely.

### 2. Orbex Prime — Stage 1 powerpack test facility and propulsion development
**Priority:** Strong secondary case study within **Propulsion & test**.

This is distinct from HURACAN: it shows test-facility and fluidic-system design, turbopump and bench support, procurement and supplier coordination, cryogenic LOX/BioLPG testing, anomaly resolution, and RCS development using RPA and Simcenter Amesim. A concise sub-study makes the breadth of the work easier to notice than burying it in a long HURACAN bullet list.

**Recommended visuals:** facility or bench context, a system diagram, and a test or integration image, each captioned to distinguish design-stage from test-stage evidence.

### 3. Pulsar Fusion — 1 kW Hall-effect thruster demonstrator
**Priority:** Short supporting item, not a long standalone page unless more evidence is available.

Describe the consultancy contribution as mechanical design support for an in-orbit demonstrator payload progressing from concept toward manufacturing readiness, plus mentoring. Keep the scope clear and avoid implying ownership of the full thruster programme.

**Recommended placement:** a compact project card or a clearly separated sub-study under **Propulsion & test**.

### 4. VTC1.5 — two-axis payload motion system
**Priority:** Flagship ESA mechanism story.

Lead with progression from concept through subsystem design, integration, and TVAC qualification. The strongest concise proof points are repositioning a **50 kg** payload at **0.5 mm** spatial resolution and completing the qualification campaign. Show the test result and design contribution together.

**Recommended visuals:** final-design CAD, TVAC test hardware, and one integration image. Preserve full frames so the mechanism remains legible.

### 5. COLIBRIS — Large Space Simulator instrumentation mechanism
**Priority:** Pair with VTC1.5 under **Space hardware**, but present it as a distinct project subsection.

Emphasize principal design ownership of a 3 m CFRP mechanism from concept through CDR and manufacturing in nine months. The best result is the final mass of **33.08 kg**, **57% below the allowable hanging mass**, alongside the photogrammetry and infrared-measurement purpose.

**Recommended visuals:** final system CAD and the in-facility image. Distinguish COLIBRIS imagery from VTC1.5 imagery in captions.

### 6. YPSAT — ESA flight payload
**Priority:** Strong proof of flight hardware experience.

Highlight structural design contribution, final-component delivery, cleanroom integration, assembly procedures, vibration-test profiles and data processing, and the shipping container for transport to Kourou. State clearly that YPSAT flew on Ariane 6's maiden flight in July 2024 and achieved its scientific objectives.

### 7. AstroBit — educational payload operated on the ISS
**Priority:** Pair with YPSAT under **Flight payloads**, with a separate subheading.

Focus on the mechanical flight case: design, build, and test; mass below **0.8 kg**; and retained access to USB and two AA batteries. Note that ESA astronaut Andreas Mogensen operated it aboard the ISS during the Huginn mission in 2023. Keep your mechanical contribution distinct from the payload's educational mission.

### 8. Concrete Canvas — production equipment redesign and manufacturing R&D
**Priority:** One of the four homepage features; it adds industrial design and implementation evidence.

Keep this as one case study with clear sub-sections:

- **Accumulator upgrade:** the CV reports a **100% capacity increase to 25 kN at 30% of replacement cost**.
- **Test verification:** the placement report records results within **25 kg of theory on a 400 kg test load** (about **6.3%** of the test load). Keep this distinct from the accumulator capacity figure.
- **Production equipment:** a **750 L hopper**, motor/gearbox and torque-arm sizing, and a full extrusion-machine assembly modelled in **four weeks**.
- **Manufacturing development:** **five design-build-test cycles**, with hands-on fabrication and production-line integration.

**Recommended visuals:** accumulator before/after or CAD, bracket FEA or fabrication, and one hopper or extrusion-machine image. Use a small gallery with descriptive captions rather than a dense image dump.

### 9. C-Star / Cyclone — 1.5 kN student bipropellant engine
**Priority:** Lead item in the university propulsion cluster; especially useful for early-career or propulsion applications.

Describe co-founding and project leadership, the move from concept toward system build, and the successful 2022 hot-fire. Include the feed, control, ignition, data-acquisition, and stand work as supporting evidence of system-level execution.

### 10. HYDRA — 0.3 kN hybrid engine
**Priority:** Supporting technical result under **Cranfield propulsion**.

Keep it to a short subsection: lead propulsion role and successful hot-fire at UK Race2Space. Do not let it blur into the separate HYROE-1 design study.

### 11. HYROE-1 — 350 N hybrid engine and test stand
**Priority:** Supporting design-stage project under **Cranfield propulsion**.

State that the engine and stand progressed through critical design review. Label it as a design-stage project; do not present it as a built or flown engine.

### 12. AETHER-I — recoverable microsatellite launch vehicle concept
**Priority:** Compact academic concept study.

Emphasize structural-workstream leadership, launcher sizing and maximum-dynamic-pressure assessment. The proposed mission capability—up to **200 kg to a 500 km polar orbit**—is a concept-study result, not a demonstrated launch capability. Keep that status explicit.

### 13. Edensat-7 — 3U CubeSat concept
**Priority:** Short student-design entry.

Mention the 48-hour UKSEDS Spaceport Cornwall hackathon and the team's 2021 win. Label it as a concept produced during the event, not a flown CubeSat.

### 14. Mach-22 — sounding-rocket payload
**Priority:** Short student flight project.

Give it a separate line from Edensat-7: payload-design leadership and contribution to integration, with the team launch reaching a 3 km apogee. This is a useful contrast between a rapid concept exercise and a launched student payload.

### 15. Cardiff University — renewable-system thesis and component-design coursework
**Priority:** Supporting academic work at the end of the portfolio.

Keep the thesis and coursework distinct: the thesis was a simulation study of a wind-solar-battery system for water pumping and communications; the coursework demonstrates CAD, FEA, mesh sensitivity, tolerancing, and drawing practice. State that the system result was simulated, not physically demonstrated. Because some loose coursework images are not mapped to exact assignments, caption them as selected BEng coursework rather than assigning unsupported project names.

## Recommended site hierarchy

1. **Propulsion & test** — HURACAN first; Orbex as a separate sub-study; Pulsar as a compact item. Keep Cranfield propulsion together in its own case study with separate C-Star, HYDRA, and HYROE-1 headings.
2. **Space hardware** — one motion-systems case study with distinct VTC1.5 and COLIBRIS sections; one flight-payloads case study with distinct YPSAT and AstroBit sections.
3. **Industrial design** — Concrete Canvas, organized around accumulator, production equipment, and manufacturing cycles.
4. **Academic & concepts** — AETHER-I; Edensat-7 and Mach-22; Cardiff University work.

For every case study, use the same reading sequence: **project and period → one-sentence summary → role → challenge → three to five contribution points → outcome → selected images**. Put the most informative image first, use later images to show analysis, integration, or test, and keep captions specific about what is shown.

## Editorial checks before publishing

- Keep team achievements separate from your own workshare.
- Label design concepts, simulations, tests, and flown hardware accurately.
- Preserve the CV/source distinction for the Concrete Canvas figures: **25 kN** comes from the CV; the **25 kg on 400 kg** measurement comes from the placement report.
- Use high-resolution source files, preserve image aspect ratios, and avoid cropping CAD labels, test fixtures, or complete mechanisms.
- Where there is no project-specific image, use a clearly labeled overview graphic rather than a generic image presented as project hardware.
